A retreat shaped by the mountain.

Sankri is presented as a mountain-resort project where compact forms, steep roofs and a strong connection to the surrounding terrain define the visual language.

The architecture is intended to feel warm in a cold landscape — using a restrained material palette, efficient construction and carefully framed views.
